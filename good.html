i How this guide works

A digital map of recommended actions for sport clubs to be crisis-ready and/or to respond to crises. Sport clubs must stay resilient during times of crisis in order to provide sport, continuity and social support to their community during difficult times and/or when the crisis is over.

Complete the Self-Evaluation Tool before starting. Once you know which area of your sport club you need to strengthen, explore the tool below for recommended actions.

Alternatively, filter the map of recommendations below, by selecting the target group and/or crisis relevant for your club and explore the recommended actions.
