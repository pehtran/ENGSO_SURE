i How this questionaire works

This questionnaire helps sport clubs and staff understand their current resilience and preparedness for a crisis.

Read and answer all questions honestly using a scale of 1-5, where 1 = Strongly disagree and 5 = Strongly agree. Questions are presented in a random order.

Once you have completed all questions, you will get a report on which areas of the club are most resilient and which need strengthened. Click ‘Download’ to refer to these results again later.

You can click on the links in the report to explore practical recommendations and actions via the Crisis Resilience Guide.

Please note: the tool will not save your responses or progress.
