i How this questionaire works

A digital questionnaire to help sport clubs and staff understand their current preparedness for a crisis. The tool looks to understand which aspects of the sport club are resilient and which need strengthening.

Read and answer all questions honestly using a scale of 1-5, where 1 = Not at all in place/Strongly disagree and 5 = Fully in place/Strongly agree.

Questions are presented in a random order. The tool will not save your information or progress so complete all questions in one sitting.

Once you have completed all questions, a spider diagram of your results will show you which areas of the club are most resilient and which need strengthened. Click ‘Download’ to refer to these results again later.

You can click on the links in the report to explore practical recommendations and actions via the Resilient Club Guide.
